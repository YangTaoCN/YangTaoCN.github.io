Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Tao Mail Assistant ("the App") handles information. The App is a private, single-user command-line tool operated by its owner for personal use. It has exactly one user: the owner of the Google account it is authorised against.

1. Information the App accesses

With the account owner's explicit consent, granted through Google's OAuth 2.0 authorisation flow, the App may access:

  • Email messages in the owner's mailbox (scope https://mail.google.com/), in order to read, search and summarise them, and to send replies.
  • Calendar data on the owner's own calendar (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar), in order to read and create events.

The App accesses only the account of the person who runs it. It does not access, request, or process data belonging to any other person.

2. How information is used

Accessed data is used solely to provide the App's functionality to its owner: listing, searching, reading and sending the owner's own email, and reading and creating the owner's own calendar events. No automated profiling, marketing or analytics is performed.

3. Storage and security

The App runs entirely on the owner's personal computer. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally in a configuration directory with file permissions restricted to the owner account (chmod 600). There is no remote server, no hosted database and no third-party storage. Data is not transmitted anywhere except in direct requests to Google's own APIs.

4. Sharing and disclosure

The App does not sell, rent, trade, share or transfer any information to third parties. Information is not used for advertising. It is not disclosed to anyone except as strictly required to operate the App's core functions described above, or where required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion

The App retains no server-side copies of any data. Locally cached data (such as OAuth tokens) can be deleted at any time by the owner simply by removing the local configuration files.

You may revoke the App's access to your Google Account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revocation immediately and permanently stops all further access.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children and is not made available to the public. It is used only by its owner.

8. Changes to this policy

Any changes to this Privacy Policy will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact
